EMG-based gait prediction using Koopman-inspired method

Published Ph.D. research, Arizona State University

A two-stage Koopman-based pipeline that predicts lower-limb kinematics from EMG signals, achieving 95% accuracy and stable near-future multi-step prediction.

Abstract

Accurate, near-future prediction of lower-limb kinematics from surface EMG is valuable for assistive and rehabilitation robotics, but nonlinear neuromuscular dynamics make this challenging for purely data-driven models. This work develops a two-stage, Koopman-inspired pipeline that lifts EMG signals into a space where gait dynamics evolve (near-)linearly, enabling stable multi-step prediction of lower-limb kinematics. The method achieves 95% prediction accuracy with stable near-future multi-step prediction, and is published in IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering (2026).
