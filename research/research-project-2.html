Integrating biophysical dynamics with data-driven method for BP estimation using PPG

Published Ph.D. research, Arizona State University

A physics-informed machine learning (PIML) model for blood pressure estimation from photoplethysmography (PPG) signals, integrating cardio-dynamics with nonlinear lifting and validated on 120 subjects in ICU conditions.

Abstract

Cuffless blood pressure (BP) estimation from photoplethysmography (PPG) is attractive for continuous, non-invasive monitoring, but purely data-driven models often struggle to generalize across subjects and physiological states. This work integrates biophysical cardio-dynamics with a data-driven, nonlinear lifting approach to estimate BP from PPG signals. By combining physics-informed structure with learned representations, the model achieves more interpretable and robust BP estimates. The approach was validated on 120 subjects under ICU conditions, and the results are published in Biomedical Signal Processing and Control (2026).
