About Me

I'm a Postdoctoral Associate at the University of Minnesota's Human Sensorimotor Control Lab, taking an upper-limb robot for rehab and proprioception studies from zero to demo for a university spinout company. My doctoral research modeled physiological and biomechanical signals (PPG, EMG, IMU) with Koopman-inspired and physics-informed machine learning methods to extract actionable insights for wearable and robotic platforms. I also build robotics simulations and autonomous systems independently, including deploying reinforcement learning policies on quadrupeds and creating a vehicle digital twin with a live data pipeline.
